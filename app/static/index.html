Follow the money to where legal process can be served

This tool follows stolen or scammed funds across the public blockchain until they reach a business (usually a crypto exchange) that can be served with a subpoena, warrant or freeze request for the account holder's identity. It never guesses: anything that is an inference rather than an on-chain fact is labelled with its confidence and basis, and every data pull is recorded for court.

    👁 Watched wallets

    Watched wallets are re-checked automatically (interval in Settings) and raise an alert when their activity changes — the signal to re-trace and serve process before funds move again. Checks compare transaction count and balance. Ethereum limitation: an incoming token-only transfer changes neither the nonce nor the ETH balance, so it may not trigger an alert until something else moves.

    🚩 Flagged wallets

    Wallets this agency has designated as fraudulent. Every future trace (any case) automatically checks against this list — when traced funds reach a flagged wallet, it is raised as a high-priority finding and marked on the map. Flags are the agency's own designations, and reports say so.

    IC3 Internet Crime Complaint — filing helper

    This worksheet mirrors the official FBI complaint form at complaint.ic3.gov. IC3 accepts complaints only through that form — this tool cannot submit for you, but it organises every answer so filing takes minutes. Do not enter Social Security numbers or dates of birth (IC3 says not to provide them). A complaint cannot be edited after it is filed.
